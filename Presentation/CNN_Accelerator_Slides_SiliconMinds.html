00Why a dedicated accelerator

Convolution is the cost.
Fixed-function is the answer.

01

The convolution layer, a sliding-window multiply-accumulate, is the most compute- and memory-intensive stage of almost every CNN vision pipeline.

02

On power- and area-constrained edge devices, a fixed datapath beats general-purpose compute on throughput per watt, per logic cell, for exactly this one operation.

This projectBuild that datapath, to the IEEE SSCS Egypt 2026 specification, and prove every number with a routed report, not an estimate.

03The MAC Engine

18 multiplies. Zero DSP.

Each output window needs 9 products; two overlapping windows a pixel apart share no reusable products, the shift pairs every pixel with a different coefficient. mac_pair.v registers all 18 products, then reduces each lane through a balanced 4-level adder tree.
The deliberate alternativeSharing a few DSP48 slices over a second, faster clock was built and simulated first (next section), this fabric datapath is what actually closed timing.
